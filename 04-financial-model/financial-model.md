## 1. Business case

_Why this initiative is worth funding over the alternatives. Include the key unit economics assumptions, CAC, LTV, payback period, where relevant._

| Assumption | Value | Source / rationale |
|---|---|---|
| CAC | $8,500 | Blended cost to acquire a mid-market GC account ($5M–$50M revenue) via field-led trials + sales-assist, lower than enterprise CAC since land motion starts with a super/foreman, not a procurement cycle |
| LTV | $42,000 | Based on average mid-market contract value (~$14K ACV) × estimated 3-year average retention once field adoption sticks |
| Payback period | ~9 months | CAC recovered from subscription revenue within three quarters, standard SaaS benchmark for a healthy B2B motion |
| Investment required | $1.2M | Engineering, design, and PM capacity for the field-adoption build (mobile app, offline sync, daily-log simplification) over two quarters |
| Expected return | 5:1 LTV:CAC | Reflects the field-first bet paying back through mid-market volume, not just enterprise ACV |

> **The case in one paragraph:** Meridian's enterprise base is stable but plateauing, while mid-market accounts are actively churning to simpler, mobile-first competitors — a segment with lower CAC and faster payback than enterprise sales-led growth. Investing in field adoption doesn't just fix a usability gap; it unlocks a materially cheaper growth motion, and it protects the enterprise base by preventing Meridian's core value (system of record) from feeling disconnected from how work actually happens on site.

## 2. Kill criteria

_The specific signals that would tell you this bet is no longer worth pursuing. Be explicit about the metric, the threshold, and the timeline._

> If **field DAU** does not reach **25% of active field users** (roughly halfway to the 55% target) by **month 6**, we will **reassess whether the field-adoption thesis is correct before committing further engineering investment, rather than continuing to fund the build on faith.**
